## Now

At **Waseela**, on the Frappe/ERPNext platform that runs an agriculture business across Pakistan:

- Led the October 2026 release of the dairy home-delivery module: a Flutter rider app and its backend, covering day close-out, prepaid deliveries with photo proof and per-day subscriptions.
- Built the engine that pays riders per kilometre from raw phone GPS. It drops inaccurate, mock-location and impossible-speed fixes and checks the track against the stop path before locking the figure.
- One of two main developers on the HR app: geofenced attendance, performance reviews, payroll settlement for leavers.

Alongside that, I own the backend of **DuoFrame**, a home-screen photo widget app live on Google Play, and led its 2026 redesign.

## How I work

- I own a feature from the database to the screen, and stay with it in production: code review, Sentry, postmortems when something breaks.
- I design for the person on the other end: field officers on weak signal, riders on entry-level phones.
- I use AI coding agents (Claude Code, Cursor) every day, and review and test everything they write.
